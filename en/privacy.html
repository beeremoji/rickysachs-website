2. General information on data processing

This website collects and processes personal data only to the extent necessary to provide a functioning website and its content and services. Analytics services (Google Analytics) are used only with your explicit consent (cookie banner).

3. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The static files of this website are provided via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When the website is accessed, the hosting provider automatically records the following data in server log files:

  • IP address of the requesting computer
  • Date and time of the request
  • Referrer URL
  • Name and URL of the file accessed
  • Browser and operating system used

This data cannot be attributed to specific persons and is not combined with other data sources. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the technical provision of the website). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Privacy information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Booking form (Vercel + HubSpot)

The booking request form on this website transmits the data you enter via an encrypted HTTPS connection to a serverless function operated on the infrastructure of Vercel (Vercel Inc., 340 S Lemon Ave #4133, Walnut, CA 91789, USA). The function then automatically creates the data in the CRM system HubSpot.

The following data is processed:

  • Name (required)
  • Email address (required)
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Type of event, date, location/venue, number of guests, PA/sound system, indoor/outdoor
  • Free-text message (optional)

Processing by Vercel: The data is processed only briefly in order to forward it to HubSpot and is not stored permanently on Vercel infrastructure. Vercel provides GDPR-compliant data processing; data is transferred to the USA on the basis of the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR). Privacy information: vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Processing by HubSpot: The booking data is stored as a contact and request in HubSpot CRM and used to handle and follow up on the request. Provider: HubSpot Ireland Limited, 2nd Floor 30 North Wall Quay, Dublin 1, Ireland. The HubSpot account used is hosted on EU servers. Privacy information: legal.hubspot.com/privacy-policy.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (steps taken at the request of the data subject prior to entering into a contract). The data is used exclusively to handle the booking request. Alternatively, you can contact me directly by email: booking@rickysachs.com

4a. Spam protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

To protect the forms (newsletter, booking request) against automated submissions ("bots"), the service Cloudflare Turnstile (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) is used. The script is only loaded when you click on or type into a form on this website. Technical data from your browser and your IP address are transmitted to Cloudflare to check whether the input comes from a human. No puzzle or image is shown as long as the check in the background is successful.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in securing the forms and preventing abuse) and § 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Privacy information: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

4b. Email delivery (Resend)

When you submit a booking request, the request is additionally sent as an email notification to the operator of this website. The service Resend (Resend, Inc., USA) is used for sending. The content of your request (name, email address, phone number if provided, event details and message) is briefly transmitted via Resend's servers.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (handling your request). The transfer to the USA is based on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR). Privacy information: resend.com/legal/privacy-policy.
